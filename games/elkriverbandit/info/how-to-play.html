How to play

You’re a trash-raiding bandit running down the Elk River. Collect loot, dodge hazards, and in Spelling mode grab the glowing letters in order to spell each word.

Spelling mode

Paste a comma-separated word list (for example river, bandit, raccoon) into the box on the title screen. Parents and teachers can use any list they like.

Tips

  • Slide under low hazards and jump gaps.
  • Pick a favourite song — or leave it on shuffle — in ⚙️ Settings.
  • Check ⚙️ → Stats for achievements.
