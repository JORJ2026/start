# Release Notes — Field Edition 1.0.0

- Twenty-operation campaign across ten named Pelagos shores.
- Eight specialists, four-person team selection, persistent injuries, role actions, tactical movement/fire/objectives/extraction, and five hostile behaviors.
- Seeded terrain, weighted movement, A* hostile routing, line-of-sight, deterministic combat rolls.
- Local supplies, three ten-node doctrine branches, field kit, medical care, resource exchanges, 30 milestones, recovered records, three difficulties and saves.
- Original atmospheric cover/icon art, local procedural wind ambience and mechanical field-action sound.
- Pause/settings/controls, field notes, campaign record, replay and responsive offline UI.

## Known limitations
The full campaign was not completed in this QA session; total balance, full-run pacing and final-operation workflow remain unverified. Tactical maps share a 12 × 9 grammar; distinction comes from authored brief, conditions, enemy mix and terrain palette rather than ten hand-built maps. Saves are local and not exportable. No multiplayer, native executable, controller remapping, voice, external cloud save or fully semantic canvas reader. System fonts vary by device.
