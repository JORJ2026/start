# Release notes — 1.0

Deep Current is a complete four-depth expedition game. Pilot through the trench, use limited sonar to locate three fragments per dive, conserve oxygen, and reach each exit. After each of the first three depths, choose one of the available adaptations. The final signal ends the expedition with a replay option.

Includes desktop keyboard, pointer sonar, mobile touch controls, pause/retry, local progress and audio preferences, a procedural ambience/effects bed, credits, and controls reference.

No known game-breaking defects remain from the checks performed. Full completion, save/continue after a browser restart, mobile hardware, and long-session performance were not exhaustively verified.
